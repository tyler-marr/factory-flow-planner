Version: 0.10.0
Date: 2026-10-31
  Features:
    - Splits and merges use splitters: a second link out of an output leaves the trunk through a splitter; a second link into an input joins through one.
    - Planner splitters: copies of every splitter in the game (vanilla, Space Age, modded) used only on the board. They don't split 50/50: the trunk keeps its items and each branch belt runs at its own planned rate.
    - The trunk belt is sized for everything that runs along it.
  Changes:
    - With no room for a splitter, links fall back to sharing the trunk or sideloading as before. Pipes join without splitters.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-31
  Features:
    - Links are real belts (items) and pipes (fluids) on the board, routed round cards and each other.
    - Each belt is the slowest tier that carries its rate, read from the game (modded belts too). Too much for the fastest belt shows "×N".

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - Supply panels (things coming in from outside, outputs on the right) and output panels (things leaving, inputs on the left). Each item can be Any, Up to or Exactly a rate.
    - Add panels from the toolbar, from the first choice in the recipe picker, or by dragging a port onto a panel.
    - Scroll over a card part to step it, as in the GTNH planner: machine count (Any, Up to, Exactly), machine, modules, beacons, and output rates. Ctrl and Shift give bigger steps. Scrolling anywhere else zooms.
    - Click a card part to step it up, right-click to step it down. Clicking the machine count cycles Any, Up to, Exactly.
    - Modules and beacons on every card, read from the game (modded ones work), with Space Age's beacon falloff.
    - Machine limits: "Up to N machines" caps a card, and whatever it can't make shows as short downstream.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - Drag and drop on the board: drag a card's title to move it, drag between an output and an input to wire them.
    - Drag an output onto empty space to add a recipe that uses it; drag an input onto empty space to add one that makes it.
    - A ghost card or wire follows the cursor while dragging.
    - Card titles show the recipe icon and "count x machine icon + name"; port icons are now clearly visible.
    - The board has a plain floor and hides the quickbar, minimap, research and hint boxes while you are on it.
  Bugfixes:
    - Icons were drawn underneath card backgrounds and almost invisible.
    - A wired card with no target could consume another card's target output.
  Changes:
    - Requires Factorio 2.1.22 or newer (drag events).
